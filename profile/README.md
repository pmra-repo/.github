# Pharmacy and Medicines Regulatory Authority (PMRA)

The **Pharmacy and Medicines Regulatory Authority (PMRA)** is Malawi’s national regulatory authority responsible for regulating medicines, allied substances, pharmaceutical businesses, and the practice of pharmacy in Malawi. PMRA was established under the PMRA Act No. 9 of 2019.

This GitHub organization hosts PMRA digital platforms, public technology initiatives, APIs, open standards, internal tooling, and collaborative software projects that support healthcare regulation and pharmaceutical governance in Malawi.

---

## 🇲🇼 About PMRA

PMRA safeguards public health through the regulation of:

* Medicines and pharmaceuticals
* Medical devices
* Cosmetics and allied substances
* Traditional and complementary medicines
* Pharmacy practice and professionals
* Pharmaceutical businesses and licensing
* Medicine quality assurance and safety monitoring

The Authority works to ensure that medicines and healthcare products available in Malawi are **safe, effective, and of high quality**. ([pmra.mw][2])


## Mission

To safeguard the health of the population of Malawi through assurance of the quality, safety, and efficacy of medicines and allied substances; and enforcement of standards in pharmacy practice. ([pmra.mw][2])


## Vision

The trusted and independent regulator of medicines, allied substances and the pharmacy profession in Malawi.

## Security

Security and confidentiality are critical to PMRA systems.

If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it responsibly through official PMRA communication channels rather than opening a public issue.
